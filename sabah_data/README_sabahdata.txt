* Data sources for the example datasets in this folder - Protecting additional rainforest in Sabah

The simple example analysis is based on:
Williams, S.H., Scriven, S.A., Burslem, D.F.R.P., Hill, J.K., Reynolds, G., Agama, A.L., Kugan, F., Maycock, C.R., Khoo, E., Hastie, A.Y.L., Sugau, J.B., Nilus, R., Pereira, J.T., Tsen, S.L.T., Lee, L.Y., Juiling, S., Hodgson, J.A., Cole, L.E.S., Asner, G.P., Evans, L.J. and Brodie, J.F. (2020), Incorporating connectivity into conservation planning for the optimal representation of multiple species and ecosystem services. Conservation Biology, 34: 934-942. https://doi.org/10.1111/cobi.13450

The data preparation was performed by Jenny Hodgson and Sarah Scriven

The file ForestEx1.tif originates from Gaveau et al 2016; their 30 m resolution data was made available under a Creative Commons License for _non-commercial_ use. We aggregated the data to 990 m resolution.

The file SourceTarget1.tif originates from selecting two protected areas from a shapefile from the World Database on Protected areas: see www.protectedplanet.net for latest versions.

The files Forestdrop.tif and Forestundrop.tif were derived by selecting unprotected and protected pixels, respectively, from the Gaveau et al 2016 data.

Gaveau, D.L.A., Sheil, D., Husnayaen, Salim, M.A., Arjasakusuma, S., Ancrenaz, M., Pacheco, P. & Meijaard, E. (2016) Rapid conversions and avoided deforestation: examining four decades of industrial plantation expansion in Borneo. Scientific Reports, 6: 32017. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep32017
